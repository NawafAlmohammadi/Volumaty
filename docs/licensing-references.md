# Licensing design references

The owner requested individual use and development with free sharing, prior written permission for every Company regardless of size, and prior written permission for selling original or modified copies. These requirements are expressed in the custom Volumaty Community License 1.0, not by relabeling a standard license.

References checked on 2026-10-23:

- [Open Source Initiative, Open Source Definition](https://opensource.org/osd): standard open-source criteria include free redistribution and no business-field restrictions. The project is described as source-available.
- [PolyForm Noncommercial 1.0.0](https://polyformproject.org/licenses/noncommercial/1.0.0): a useful comparison, but its permitted organizational uses do not express the owner's company-permission policy exactly. The Volumaty license is not presented as an unmodified PolyForm license.
- [GitHub Docs, Licensing a repository](https://docs.github.com/en/repositories/managing-your-repositorys-settings-and-features/customizing-your-repository/licensing-a-repository): place the license at the repository root and distinguish a public repository from an open-source license.
- [GitHub Terms of Service, sections D.3–D.8](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/github-terms/github-terms-of-service#d-user-generated-content): independently granted platform permissions, including viewing/forking public repositories, remain separate.

The license text is project-specific and has not been independently reviewed by legal counsel. No claim is made that it guarantees enforcement in every jurisdiction. Third-party license texts remain unchanged.
